# Concept gaps: what the 13 scenario specs rely on that the panel does not measure

Source: a full reading of `scenariolab/scenarios/specs/*.yaml` (summary, narrative, causal_chain, view reasons, overrides). Generated by `concept_gaps.py` (numbers and scenario lists in `concept_gaps.json`). Every scenario listed under a concept was checked to contain one of the concept's keywords; the keyword hits that were judged not to be the concept (e.g. 房地产 = the REIT asset) are listed in the JSON.

## Summary

* The 13 causal chains have **117 distinct nodes**. 39 are measured by a panel variable, 33 only by a partial proxy (an aggregate, an EWMA proxy, an endogenous series or industrial production standing in for GDP), and **45 have no panel variable at all** (appendix).
* Views use only 22 panel columns: `s_cpi_yoy` (13), `s_ip_yoy` (10), `f_unrate` (8), `f_capex_share` (5), `f_oil` (5), `s_unrate` (4), `f_ff` (4), `s_spread` (4), `s_ff` (3), `f_usd` (3), `s_productivity_yoy` (2), `f_profit_share` (2), `s_div_yield` (2), `s_goods_yoy` (2), `f_sentiment` (2), `s_real_ff` (2), `f_y10` (2), `s_services_yoy` (1), `s_sentiment` (1), `f_spread` (1), `e_oil_supply` (1), `s_real_wage_yoy` (1).
* Where a concept is missing, the specs carry it with overrides: `asset_drift: energy, utilities` (energy_transition); `asset_drift: financials, nasdaq, real_estate, us_equity` (financial_crisis, japanification); `asset_drift: nasdaq, us_equity` (ai_bust); `calibration (income-distribution calculator)` (agi_boom, k_shaped); `factor_drift: ip` (agi_boom); `factor_drift: unrate` (k_shaped); `r_star` (deglobalization, financial_crisis, fiscal_dominance, japanification, reacceleration, stagflation); `reversion.kappa_ff` (stagflation); `reversion.kappa_y10` (dollar_decline); `reversion.oil_target_shift` (energy_transition); `reversion.term_premium` (dollar_decline).
* The concepts that matter most, by the miss map and the user's questions, and that have an allowed monthly source covering 1973 on (so they could become core transmission factors, not just states):
  1. **risk appetite / financial conditions** (excess bond premium 1973+, NFCI 1971+): the miss map's PC1, 56% of the OOS residual variance; it is also what the asset_drift overrides of ai_bust, financial_crisis and japanification stand in for.
  2. **term premium** (ACM 1961+): the dollar_decline override, and the y10 beta flip around 2000.
  3. **household real income and consumption** (BEA monthly 1959+): 8 specs reason through consumption, the panel has no consumption or real-income state; this is the household-income feedback the user asks about.
  4. **white-collar earnings** (CES supersector earnings, 1960s+, check): k_shaped and agi_boom run on a calculator with no data behind the white-collar income path.
  5. **power demand** (G.17 electric utilities 1972+) and **AI-capex detail** (NIPA information processing and software investment, quarterly 1947+): utilities and nasdaq drift in the AI era.
* Concepts with a long allowed source that are better kept as slow states or view targets: tariffs (NIPA customs duties / imports), fiscal deficit and debt, house prices (1975+), inflation expectations (1978+), r* (1961+), geopolitical risk (1985+), unit labor costs, real GDP.
* No allowed source: central-bank gold buying (WGC / IMF), political pressure on the Fed, AI adoption itself, private credit, stranded assets. These stay overrides or narrative; the UI should say so.
* No spec mentions China (中国需求 appears only in the china_credit era text); a global-demand factor (Kilian index 1968+) is still worth a test for energy and industrials.

Role codes: CORE = candidate core transmission factor (needs every month 1973-02..today, check with `harness.coverage`); DED = dedicated factor for one or two assets; SLOW = slow state (test with `residual_test(horizons=(1, 12))` and `predictive_test`, use in views); VIEW = view target or override calibration only; CAL = calibration input; NONE = no allowed long source. Start years are the publisher's; items marked 'check' were not verified here. Verify every FRED ID before use.

## A. 贸易与全球化

### 关税 / 有效关税率 — tariffs, effective tariff rate

* **Scenarios (2):** deglobalization, stagflation
* **Approximated today:** No variable. deglobalization picks months by goods inflation (s_goods_yoy 5.5) and IP (0.0); the pass-through is hand arithmetic in the narrative (about 15% effective tariff x imports about 1/3 of goods consumption). stagflation names tariffs in its summary and the causal node 油价与关税, but its views carry only oil (f_oil +15%/yr).
* **Candidate series:** Effective tariff rate = customs duties / goods imports: customs duties from the Monthly Treasury Statement (api.fiscaldata.treasury.gov; FRED has the MTS receipts) or NIPA federal customs duties (BEA via FRED, quarterly 1947+); goods imports (NIPA quarterly 1947+, Census monthly 1990s+). Trade policy uncertainty: Caldara-Iacoviello et al. TPU (matteoiacoviello.com, monthly 1985+; a longer historical version exists, check) and the EPU trade-policy category (policyuncertainty.com, 1985+).
* **History:** quarterly 1947+ (tariff rate); TPU 1985+. **Role:** VIEW / SLOW.
* **Note:** Post-1973 history has few tariff episodes (2018-19, 2025-26), so a tariff factor cannot be estimated as monthly transmission. Use the effective rate as a state and a calibrated goods-inflation view; TPU as an uncertainty factor (1985+, not core).

### 进口价格 — import prices

* **Scenarios (3):** deglobalization, dollar_decline, stagflation
* **Approximated today:** Through s_goods_yoy and f_usd views; no import price series.
* **Candidate series:** BLS import price indexes (all imports, nonfuel; api.bls.gov or FRED, monthly from the 1980s, check start); NIPA import deflator (BEA via FRED, quarterly 1947+).
* **History:** monthly 1980s+ / quarterly 1947+. **Role:** SLOW / VIEW.
* **Note:** Mechanism variable between usd / tariffs and goods inflation; mostly a view target.

### 出口、贸易报复、贸易结算 — exports, retaliation, trade settlement

* **Scenarios (2):** deglobalization, dollar_decline
* **Approximated today:** s_ip_yoy view only (deglobalization); dollar_decline's settlement shift has no variable.
* **Candidate series:** Real exports of goods (NIPA quarterly 1947+; Census monthly 1990s+, via FRED); OECD composite leading indicators of trading partners (sdmx.oecd.org, monthly 1960s+ for major economies).
* **History:** quarterly 1947+. **Role:** SLOW.

### 供应链压力 / 回流 — supply-chain pressure, reshoring

* **Scenarios (1):** deglobalization
* **Approximated today:** Phase-2 views on f_capex_share (+0.25/yr) and s_productivity_yoy (1.0); the 2021-22 months enter through goods inflation.
* **Candidate series:** NY Fed Global Supply Chain Pressure Index (newyorkfed.org, monthly 1997+, revised); Fed G.17 capacity utilization (1967+); Census manufacturing construction spending (FRED, 1990s+).
* **History:** 1997+. **Role:** VIEW / SLOW.
* **Note:** Too short for a core factor.

### 中国需求 / 全球需求 — China and global demand

* **Scenarios (0):** none
* **Approximated today:** No spec names China (0 of 13). It appears only in the era description china_credit (中国冲击). Global demand reaches the model only through oil, the dollar and US IP.
* **Candidate series:** Kilian index of global real economic activity (Dallas Fed via FRED, monthly 1968+; built from freight rates, so run endogeneity_check); World Bank Pink Sheet metals and energy indexes (worldbank.org, monthly 1960+); OECD CLI for China (sdmx.oecd.org, check start).
* **History:** 1968+ (Kilian). **Role:** CORE? (energy, industrials, gold).
* **Note:** A missing global-demand factor is plausible for energy and industrials (oil price moves are demand or supply); the SVAR's e_demand shock is a US demand shock.

## B. 财政与主权债务

### 财政赤字与利息支出 — fiscal deficit, interest outlays

* **Scenarios (4):** dollar_decline, fiscal_dominance, reacceleration, stagflation
* **Approximated today:** No variable. r_star overrides (fiscal_dominance -2.5 / -1.0, reacceleration 1.5 / 1.0, stagflation -1.0 / 3.0) and the dollar_decline term-premium override carry the fiscal story; the narrative's deficit (6-7% of GDP) and interest-outlay numbers are not in the panel.
* **Candidate series:** Monthly federal surplus/deficit and interest outlays (Monthly Treasury Statement: api.fiscaldata.treasury.gov; FRED monthly from 1980-10); NIPA federal interest payments and net lending (BEA via FRED, quarterly 1947+). Use 12-month sums / GDP: the monthly deficit is dominated by tax-calendar seasonality.
* **History:** quarterly 1947+, monthly 1980+. **Role:** SLOW.
* **Note:** A slow state for views and the policy rule; test with residual_test(horizons=(1,12)) and predictive_test (y10, ff, usd targets).

### 国债供给 / 债务/GDP — Treasury supply, debt/GDP

* **Scenarios (2):** fiscal_dominance, stagflation
* **Approximated today:** Narrative only (fiscal_dominance 债务被通胀稀释; stagflation scales the Volcker real rate by today's debt level).
* **Candidate series:** Federal debt held by the public / GDP (FRED, quarterly from the 1960s, check start); Treasury Monthly Statement of the Public Debt (fiscaldata, monthly 2000s+).
* **History:** quarterly 1960s+. **Role:** SLOW.

### QE / 央行资产负债表 / 金融抑制 — QE, central-bank balance sheet, financial repression

* **Scenarios (2):** financial_crisis, fiscal_dominance
* **Approximated today:** r_star overrides and the 10-year rule (fiscal_dominance: 10y converges to inflation - 1.75).
* **Candidate series:** Fed total assets (H.4.1 via FRED, weekly 2002-12+); Fed Treasury holdings and reserves (FRED, longer); commercial-bank Treasury and agency holdings (H.8 via FRED, 1973+).
* **History:** 2002+ (balance sheet). **Role:** VIEW.
* **Note:** QE exists only since 2008, so its transmission cannot be estimated before; keep it a view.

### 对央行的政治压力 — political pressure on the Fed

* **Scenarios (2):** fiscal_dominance, stagflation
* **Approximated today:** r_star overrides (fiscal_dominance -2.5, stagflation -1.0) and kappa_ff (stagflation).
* **Candidate series:** No allowed long series. Closest: EPU monetary-policy category (policyuncertainty.com, 1985+).
* **History:** none. **Role:** NONE / VIEW.
* **Note:** Remains an override; say so in the UI.

## C. 利率与预期

### 期限溢价 — term premium

* **Scenarios (1):** dollar_decline
* **Approximated today:** reversion.term_premium 1.75 (default 0.75) and kappa_y10 doubled in dollar_decline; the panel's s_term_spread (10Y - FF) mixes expected short rates and the premium.
* **Candidate series:** ACM term premium (newyorkfed.org, daily/monthly 1961+); Kim-Wright term premium (federalreserve.gov, daily 1990+; on FRED).
* **History:** 1961+ (ACM). **Role:** CORE candidate.
* **Note:** Also the mechanism behind the miss map's y10 beta flip around 2000 (split Δy10 into expected path and premium). ACM is a model fitted on the full sample: the decomposition is revised, so check a real-time version or accept the caveat.

### 中性利率 r* — neutral real rate

* **Scenarios (8):** baseline, deglobalization, dollar_decline, financial_crisis, fiscal_dominance, japanification, reacceleration, stagflation
* **Approximated today:** A constant 0.75 in the engine's policy rule; six specs override it (deglobalization 0.25, financial_crisis -1.5, fiscal_dominance -2.5/-1.0, japanification -0.5/-1.0, reacceleration 1.5/1.0, stagflation -1.0/3.0).
* **Candidate series:** Holston-Laubach-Williams / Laubach-Williams estimates (newyorkfed.org, quarterly 1961+; one-sided estimates are the real-time ones).
* **History:** quarterly 1961+. **Role:** SLOW (policy-rule state).
* **Note:** An anchor for the rate rule and a check on the overrides, not a transmission factor.

### 通胀预期 / 通胀中枢 — inflation expectations

* **Scenarios (2):** stagflation, deglobalization
* **Approximated today:** s_infl_expect = EWMA of past CPI inflation (half-life 36 months), used for s_real_y10 and the valuation module; stagflation picks months by CPI, real wages and the real policy rate instead.
* **Candidate series:** Michigan 1-year expectations (sca.isr.umich.edu, monthly 1978+) and 5-10-year (monthly 1990+); SPF CPI 1-year (1981+) and 10-year (1991+), Livingston (semiannual 1946+) (philadelphiafed.org); Cleveland Fed expected inflation (FRED, monthly 1982+); TIPS breakevens (H.15, 2003+).
* **History:** monthly 1978+; semiannual 1946+. **Role:** CORE candidate (spliced) / SLOW.
* **Note:** Starts after 1973-02, so a core factor needs an honest splice (Livingston interpolated is NOT honest: hold it from survey to survey). Also gives a survey-based real yield.

### 实际收益率（TIPS） — real yields

* **Scenarios (1):** fiscal_dominance
* **Approximated today:** s_real_y10 / f_real_y10 = y10 - EWMA inflation (a proxy, spanned by the core factors: endogeneity_check r2_core 0.99).
* **Candidate series:** 10-year TIPS yield (federalreserve.gov H.15, 2003+); Kim-Wright / GSW TIPS curve (federalreserve.gov, 1999+).
* **History:** 1999+. **Role:** VIEW / DED (gold).
* **Note:** Too short for core; useful for gold.

## D. 信用、银行与房地产

### 风险偏好 / 金融条件 / 估值的非宏观部分 — risk appetite, financial conditions, the non-macro part of valuation

* **Scenarios (4):** financial_crisis, ai_bust, japanification, agi_boom
* **Approximated today:** s_spread (BAA-10Y) and the e_financial SVAR shock; ai_bust, financial_crisis and japanification add asset_drift (-8%/yr, the bound) because the drawn months keep only half of the valuation compression, which was almost all residual. agi_boom inherits the drawn months' P/D expansion (P/D 115x in year 7).
* **Candidate series:** Gilchrist-Zakrajsek spread and excess bond premium (federalreserve.gov csv, monthly 1973+); Chicago Fed NFCI / ANFCI and subindexes (chicagofed.org or FRED, weekly 1971+); EPU (policyuncertainty.com, 1985+); VXO / VIX (cboe.com or FRED, 1986 / 1990+); St. Louis Fed financial stress index (FRED, 1993+).
* **History:** monthly 1973+ (EBP), weekly 1971+ (NFCI). **Role:** CORE candidate.
* **Note:** The miss map's PC1 (56% of residual variance, 0.95 with the us_equity residual). EBP and NFCI contain market prices: run endogeneity_check; VIX is an option price (expect 'endogenous').

### 房价 / 房产财富 — house prices, housing wealth

* **Scenarios (2):** financial_crisis, japanification
* **Approximated today:** Nothing. s_wealth_income (household net worth / DPI) includes real estate but is endogenous and interpolated; REITs and financials get asset_drift in financial_crisis and japanification.
* **Candidate series:** Freddie Mac House Price Index (freddiemac.com, monthly 1975+); FHFA HPI (quarterly 1975+, monthly purchase-only 1991+; FRED); S&P CoreLogic Case-Shiller (FRED, 1987+, S&P copyright); Shiller's long home-price series (econ.yale.edu, annual 1890+); housing starts / permits (Census via FRED, 1959 / 1960+); Freddie Mac PMMS 30-year mortgage rate (1971+).
* **History:** monthly 1975+ (HPI); 1959+ (starts). **Role:** DED (real_estate, financials) / SLOW.
* **Note:** Repeat-sales indexes are smooth and revised: test as a slow factor (horizon 12) and with release dating (FMHPI is published with about a 2-month lag). 1973-02..1974-12 needs a proxy for a core factor (starts / permits / mortgage rate cover it).

### 私募信贷 / 商业地产 / 杠杆贷款 — private credit, CRE, leveraged loans

* **Scenarios (1):** financial_crisis
* **Approximated today:** Spread views (f_spread +1.5/yr, s_spread 3.0-3.6) and asset_drift.
* **Candidate series:** Commercial real estate price index (Fed Z.1 / DDP, quarterly; check start); CRE loan delinquency (federalreserve.gov, quarterly 1991+); no long allowed series for private credit or leveraged loans.
* **History:** 1990s+. **Role:** VIEW.

### 信贷供给 / 银行资本 / 净息差 — credit supply, bank capital, net interest margin

* **Scenarios (3):** financial_crisis, japanification, fiscal_dominance
* **Approximated today:** s_spread only; japanification's bank-margin channel is narrative.
* **Candidate series:** SLOOS net tightening (federalreserve.gov, quarterly 1990+); bank credit and loans (H.8 via FRED, 1947+ monthly / 1973+ weekly); charge-off and delinquency rates (federalreserve.gov, quarterly 1985+); net interest margin (FFIEC via FRED, quarterly 1984+); EBP (see risk appetite).
* **History:** 1973+ (H.8); 1990+ (SLOOS). **Role:** DED (financials) / SLOW.

### 企业去杠杆 / 企业债务 — corporate leverage
